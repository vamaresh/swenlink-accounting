Privacy Policy

SwenBooks helps UK small businesses and limited company directors keep accounting records, including invoices, expenses, supplier bills, banking information, director loan movements and compliance reminders.

Last updated: 7 June 2026

SwenBooks is designed for business record keeping. Do not enter personal data that is not needed for your accounting, tax, compliance or business administration purposes.

Information We Collect

When you use SwenBooks, the app may collect and store information you provide, including:

  • Account and login details, such as your email address.
  • Company profile details, including company name, registration number and accounting settings.
  • Customer and supplier records, including names, contact details and addresses.
  • Sales invoices, expenses, purchase bills, bank transactions, account codes, reconciliation notes and uploaded business records.
  • Application preferences, such as theme, font settings and workflow settings.
  • Technical information needed to run, secure and improve the service, such as browser, device, session and error information.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and improve the SwenBooks service, including to:

  • Operate accounting workflows and store business records.
  • Generate invoices, reports, reminders and reconciliation suggestions.
  • Support security, authentication, troubleshooting and service reliability.
  • Maintain legal, tax, audit and business administration records where required.
  • Respond to support requests and improve product usability.

Legal Basis

Where UK GDPR applies, we process personal data where it is necessary to provide the service, perform a contract, comply with legal obligations, support legitimate business interests, or where you have given consent.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with service providers that help operate the app, such as hosting, authentication, database, storage, analytics, email or support providers. We may also share information where required by law, regulation, court order or a lawful request from an authority.

Data Storage and Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should keep login details safe and only upload information needed for business purposes.

Retention

Business and accounting records may need to be retained for legal, tax, compliance, audit or dispute purposes. We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. You can request deletion where applicable, subject to legal and accounting retention requirements.

Your Rights

Depending on your location and the type of information involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to request a copy of your data. Some rights may be limited where information must be retained for legal, accounting or compliance reasons.

Children

SwenBooks is intended for business users and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material updates will be reflected by changing the date at the top of this page.
